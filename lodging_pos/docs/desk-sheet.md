# Desk sheet (print and keep at the desk)

One page for the supervisor and the receptionists at UB40. It adds to what the app says on screen; when the two differ, the app is right and this sheet needs updating.

## Opening and closing a shift

- **Opening:** there is no float to type. The float is what the drawer held at its last count. On the very first day, the supervisor or owner counts the starting cash first (Cash → Starting cash).
- **Closing:** count all the cash in the drawer and enter it. The screen never shows what it expects.
  - "Your count is recorded": done, sign out.
  - "Your count doesn't match. Count the cash again.": count once more, carefully. Your first count stays on the record.
  - "Your count still doesn't match": say why (e.g. "gave change from my pocket"), then close. The supervisor reviews it.
- Leaving the close screen and coming back doesn't start over: you continue where you were.

## Drawer checks and the weekly collection

- The supervisor or the owner may **check the drawer at any time**, and **collects the cash weekly**. You are there: you type **your own password** to confirm it. Nobody else may type it for you.
- They count the drawer; the screen only says "Counted." If the count doesn't match, they count again, and if it still doesn't, they write why. It is reviewed.
- At a collection they also record how much they take away. Your next float is what is left.
- A count that didn't match is reviewed: the supervisor (or the owner, for a supervisor's own shift or count) approves or rejects it on Cash. Nobody reviews their own.

## Correcting a wrong payment method or M-Pesa code

The method (Cash or M-Pesa) and the M-Pesa code can't be changed after a payment is saved. To correct them while the guest is still in the bed:

1. **Supervisor** (or the owner): Voids → find the receipt (receipt number or the guest's phone) → **Void stay…** → reason, e.g. "Paid by M-Pesa, not cash". The stay and its payments are voided; the receipt shows VOID; the bed becomes available and needs cleaning.
2. **Supervisor**: Housekeeping → mark the bed **clean** (the guest hasn't left; nothing needs cleaning).
3. **Receptionist**: check the guest in again on the same bed, with the **right** method and code. A new receipt prints; give it to the guest and keep the VOID one with the day's papers.

Nobody voids their own record: if the supervisor took the payment or checked the guest in, the owner voids it.

**Don't void the payment alone** while the guest is in the bed: the guest would stay in the bed with nights no money is behind, and Today shows "Guest in bed, payment voided" until the stay is voided too.

## What the app won't void

- **A stay with a refund** (the guest left early), or its payments.
- **A refund payout** (money paid back to a guest).

Write these in the **corrections book** (date, receipt number, what was wrong, what it should have been, who) and tell the owner.

## A void moves no money

A void says the money was never taken. If cash has to go back to a guest, that is a refund (early checkout), not a void.
