# UX profile — lodging_pos

Short context for UX reviews and audits. Product truth: `../PRODUCT.md`.
Design rules: `docs/design-system.md` (v2, being built) and
`docs/design.md` (what is built today). Decisions: `docs/ui-redesign-brief.md`.

## Product
- Front desk and owner system for one lodging (UB40) that charges per
  bed, per night, checkout 10:00.
- Kenya: KES, cash and M-Pesa (paybill), English only, Nairobi time.
- Pattern family: operational app (Operate mode). No marketing surfaces.

## Surfaces
| Surface | Platform | Size to design for | Network |
|---|---|---|---|
| Desk | Flutter, Windows | 1366×768 laptop; must work 768–1920 wide | Offline-first |
| Owner's app | Flutter, Android and web | 360×640 and 412×915 | Online only |

## Users
| Role | Where | Main jobs | Skill |
|---|---|---|---|
| Receptionist | Desk | Check in and out, take payment, print receipts, pay refunds, see free and clean beds | Moderate computer skills; repeated all day |
| Supervisor | Desk | Everything a receptionist does, setup, drawer checks and collections, cash reviews | Daily |
| Owner | Phone mostly, desk sometimes | Today, money, beds, decide refunds, prices and cash reviews, staff | Short glances on the go |

## Non-negotiables
- A receptionist never sees a cash total, a drawer balance or a review
  outcome, on any screen, list or PDF.
- Hiding is never the only lock: every screen and action is permission-checked.
- Personal data is masked in lists (`0722 ••• 310`, `••••3921`); the phone
  never downloads ID numbers or full phones.
- The system calculates prices, refunds and statuses; nobody types them.
- Nothing is deleted: corrections are visible voids.
- Status is never colour alone; text ≥ 12 px; targets ≥ 44 px; contrast
  4.5:1 for text.
- A redesign never removes a function unless the brief lists it under Dropped.

## Design direction (v2)
- Light top bar with icon tabs and a sliding indicator; Setup ▾ menu;
  tabs collapse into a menu whenever they don't fit.
- Calm by default, problems loudest: marine = brand and action,
  marine-tint = occupied, red = overstay and urgent, butter = cleaning and
  warnings, green = good, category colours identify rooms.
- Three weights (400/500/600), Manrope + IBM Plex Mono.
- Motion only to show where you went or what changed; honours reduced motion.
- Fewer words: labels and values; explanations go in training and
  `docs/desk-sheet.md`.

## Review checklist
1. Does the screen open with what needs action?
2. One job, one page pattern?
3. Could a receptionist see a total anywhere (including badges and PDFs)?
4. Every colour doing one of its jobs, and never the only signal?
5. Works at 1366×768, 1024×768 and 768×1024 without page scroll?
6. Keyboard: N, /, Esc where they apply; focus visible; Esc returns focus.
7. Anything today's screen does that the new one lost?
